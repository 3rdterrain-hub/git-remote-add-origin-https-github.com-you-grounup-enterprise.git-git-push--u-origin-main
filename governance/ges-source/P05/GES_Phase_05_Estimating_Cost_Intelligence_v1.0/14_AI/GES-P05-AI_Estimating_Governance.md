# AI Estimating Governance

AI may read plans, specifications, reports, addenda, quotes and historical data; propose quantities, line items, crews, production, risks and questions; and explain its reasoning. It shall not silently publish, approve, submit, overwrite master data, or change financial terms.

## Required controls
- Evidence references for extracted facts
- Confidence and uncertainty
- Human disposition for material recommendations
- Prompt/model/version logging
- Tenant data isolation
- Prohibited cross-tenant learning
- Reproducible calculation trace
- Feedback and calibration approval
