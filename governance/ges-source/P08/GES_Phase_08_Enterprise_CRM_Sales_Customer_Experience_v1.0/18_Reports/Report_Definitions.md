# Report Definitions

| ID | Report | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| GES-P08-RPT-001 | Executive Revenue Dashboard | Pipeline, forecast, bookings, conversion, velocity, and customer value. |
| GES-P08-RPT-002 | Lead Source Performance | Volume, quality, conversion, revenue, and cost by source/campaign. |
| GES-P08-RPT-003 | Pipeline Health | Stage value, aging, stalled deals, next actions, and risk. |
| GES-P08-RPT-004 | Sales Activity & SLA | Calls, emails, meetings, response time, follow-up compliance, and workload. |
| GES-P08-RPT-005 | Proposal Performance | Issue-to-view, view-to-accept, time to decision, revisions, and option selection. |
| GES-P08-RPT-006 | Win/Loss Analysis | Reasons, competitors, pricing, trade, geography, and customer segment. |
| GES-P08-RPT-007 | Revenue Forecast | Commit, best case, pipeline, weighted, and scenario forecast. |
| GES-P08-RPT-008 | Marketing Journey Performance | Enrollment, delivery, engagement, conversion, suppression, and attribution. |
| GES-P08-RPT-009 | Customer Health & Retention | Health distribution, risks, touchpoints, service issues, and expansion. |
| GES-P08-RPT-010 | Reputation & NPS | Survey response, NPS, review volume, rating, sentiment, and response SLA. |
| GES-P08-RPT-011 | Referral Performance | Referrals, conversion, revenue, rewards, and top advocates. |
| GES-P08-RPT-012 | Warranty & Service Experience | Request volume, eligibility, response, completion, satisfaction, and recurrence. |
