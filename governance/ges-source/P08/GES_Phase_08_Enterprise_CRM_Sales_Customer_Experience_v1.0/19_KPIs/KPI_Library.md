# KPI Library

| ID | KPI | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| GES-P08-KPI-001 | Lead Response Time | Median time from lead receipt to first human or governed automated response. |
| GES-P08-KPI-002 | Lead Qualification Rate | Qualified leads divided by processed leads. |
| GES-P08-KPI-003 | Lead-to-Opportunity Conversion | Opportunities created divided by qualified leads. |
| GES-P08-KPI-004 | Opportunity Win Rate | Won opportunities divided by closed opportunities. |
| GES-P08-KPI-005 | Pipeline Velocity | Qualified opportunities multiplied by win rate and average value, divided by sales-cycle days. |
| GES-P08-KPI-006 | Stage Aging | Average days active opportunities remain in each stage. |
| GES-P08-KPI-007 | Sales Cycle Length | Average days from qualified lead to won or lost. |
| GES-P08-KPI-008 | Average Opportunity Value | Average expected or booked value per opportunity. |
| GES-P08-KPI-009 | Proposal View Rate | Issued proposals viewed by at least one recipient. |
| GES-P08-KPI-010 | Proposal Acceptance Rate | Accepted proposals divided by issued proposals. |
| GES-P08-KPI-011 | Proposal Decision Time | Median time from issue to accept, decline, or expiration. |
| GES-P08-KPI-012 | Forecast Accuracy | Variance between forecast bookings and actual bookings. |
| GES-P08-KPI-013 | Marketing Qualified Lead Cost | Campaign cost divided by marketing-qualified leads. |
| GES-P08-KPI-014 | Campaign Revenue Attribution | Attributed booked revenue by campaign. |
| GES-P08-KPI-015 | Customer Lifetime Value | Expected gross contribution over the customer relationship. |
| GES-P08-KPI-016 | Customer Health Score | Composite score of engagement, delivery, payment, service, and sentiment. |
| GES-P08-KPI-017 | Net Promoter Score | Promoters percentage minus detractors percentage. |
| GES-P08-KPI-018 | Review Conversion Rate | Published reviews divided by eligible review requests. |
| GES-P08-KPI-019 | Referral Conversion Rate | Won referred opportunities divided by valid referrals. |
| GES-P08-KPI-020 | Warranty First Response Time | Median time from warranty submission to first accountable response. |
