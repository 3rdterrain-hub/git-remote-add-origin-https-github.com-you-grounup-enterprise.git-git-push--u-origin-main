# AI Sales Intelligence Specification

Phase 08 AI operates through Phase 06 governance. Supported functions include lead classification, conversation summarization, opportunity risk detection, proposal drafting, next-best-action ranking, forecast assistance, sentiment and intent analysis, expansion identification, and customer health explanation.

AI outputs must include confidence, source references, policy version, model route, prompt version, and approval state. AI cannot independently send external messages, alter approved prices, accept contracts, promise schedule dates, or commit warranty coverage unless a tenant has explicitly delegated that action through policy and authorization.
