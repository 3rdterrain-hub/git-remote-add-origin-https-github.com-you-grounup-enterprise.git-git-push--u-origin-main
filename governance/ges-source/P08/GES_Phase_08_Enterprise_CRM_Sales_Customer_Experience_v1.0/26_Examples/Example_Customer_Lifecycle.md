# Example Customer Lifecycle

A website drainage lead is captured with campaign attribution and consent. Identity resolution detects an existing homeowner contact. The lead is scored, assigned to the Toledo residential territory, and a site visit is scheduled. Field discovery generates an estimate request in Phase 05. The approved estimate scenario becomes a branded proposal with base work and alternates. After commercial approval, the proposal is issued and signed electronically. The award handoff creates a Phase 07 project with customer, scope, commercial terms, documents, and communication history. After completion, the customer receives a satisfaction survey and review request. A warranty request, referral, or future opportunity remains connected to the same Customer 360 record.
