# Phase 16 Deployment Guide

1. Configure companies, projects, spatial roles and project geospatial profiles.
2. Load supported CRS/datum/geoid/unit libraries and transformation controls.
3. Configure survey instrument, GNSS, drone, aircraft, payload and battery masters.
4. Configure survey QA, photogrammetry/LiDAR QA and acceptance templates.
5. Configure storage/tiling for rasters, point clouds and surfaces.
6. Configure drone mission, flight, compliance and evidence workflows by applicable jurisdiction.
7. Migrate control points, surfaces, GIS layers, historical captures and survey deliverables.
8. Validate coordinate transformations and representative survey/reality-capture calculations.
9. Validate mobile/offline GIS and survey field workflows.
10. Reconcile volumes/progress with Phase 05, Phase 07 and Phase 13.
11. Validate document references with Phase 15 and machine-control handoff with Phase 17 interface.
12. Complete Survey/GIS, Drone Operations, Architecture, Security, AI Governance, Data Governance and UAT approvals.
13. Release by controlled project waves with rollback and data-reconciliation plan.
