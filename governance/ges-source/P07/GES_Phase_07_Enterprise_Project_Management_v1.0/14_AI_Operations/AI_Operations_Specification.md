# AI Operations Specification

Phase 07 operational AI uses Phase 06 orchestration, retrieval, confidence, evidence, audit, and approval controls.

## Operational agents
- Project Manager AI: cross-module status, action preparation, meeting and executive summaries.
- Superintendent AI: daily-report review, production variance, constraints, work-plan support.
- Scheduler AI: schedule risk, critical-path movement, recovery options, and look-ahead readiness.
- Cost Control AI: forecast anomalies, cost-code trends, change exposure, and EAC scenarios.
- Document AI: classification, revision comparison, RFI/submittal extraction, and missing-document detection.
- Safety and Quality AI: pattern detection, checklist assistance, corrective-action drafting, and trend summaries.
- Procurement AI: lead-time risk, required-on-site conflicts, and alternative sourcing analysis.

## Mandatory controls
Every material recommendation must include evidence references, assumptions, confidence, affected records, expected impact, model/task version, and a clear approval state. AI may not approve contracts, budget changes, schedule baselines, payments, incident classifications, or external communications unless an authorized human approves the prepared action.
