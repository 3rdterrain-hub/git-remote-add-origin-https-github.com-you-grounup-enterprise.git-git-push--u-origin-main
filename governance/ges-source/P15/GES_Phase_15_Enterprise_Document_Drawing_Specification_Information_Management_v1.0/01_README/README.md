# GES Phase 15 - Enterprise Document, Drawing, Specification & Construction Information Management

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Review-ready  
Proposed lock: GES-P15-v1.0.0

Phase 15 establishes GrounUp's controlled construction information environment for documents, drawings, specifications, RFIs, submittals, transmittals, markups, search, AI plan/spec intelligence, field current sets, retention and closeout.

## Non-negotiable controls
- Authoritative revision control and supersession.
- Permission-aware search, preview, download, export and AI retrieval.
- File checksums and immutable audit history.
- Legal hold overrides normal deletion/disposition.
- AI answers/extractions preserve source evidence and revision context.
- AI cannot autonomously publish authoritative revisions or contractual RFI/submittal responses.
