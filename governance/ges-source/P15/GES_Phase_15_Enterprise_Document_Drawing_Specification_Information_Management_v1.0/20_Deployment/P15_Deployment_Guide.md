# Phase 15 Deployment Guide

1. Configure companies, projects, document roles and information-management profiles.
2. Configure numbering, naming, metadata, discipline and status dictionaries.
3. Configure storage, checksums, previews, OCR and search indexes.
4. Configure revision, approval, distribution, RFI, submittal and transmittal workflows.
5. Migrate registers and files with source revision history.
6. Reconcile authoritative current sets and superseded records.
7. Configure permissions, external sharing, retention and legal holds.
8. Validate field/offline current-set synchronization.
9. Evaluate AI extraction, Q&A, conflict detection and evidence citation.
10. Complete UAT, security/privacy, records governance and information-management approval.
11. Release by controlled project waves with rollback and migration reconciliation.
