# Phase 10 AI Financial Governance

AI may classify invoices, recommend coding, flag duplicate or unusual transactions, forecast cash and margin, explain variances, identify billing opportunities, and draft financial narratives. AI may not autonomously post journals, release payments, change vendor banking, close periods, recognize revenue, submit tax filings, or override approval policies. Every recommendation must include confidence, cited source records, model and prompt version, material assumptions, and an accountable human decision.
