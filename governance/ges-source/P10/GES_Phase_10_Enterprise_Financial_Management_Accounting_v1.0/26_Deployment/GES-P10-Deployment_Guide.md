# Phase 10 Deployment Guide

1. Confirm Phases 01-09 interfaces and identifiers.
2. Configure legal entities, fiscal calendars, currencies, chart of accounts, dimensions, posting profiles, approval matrices, banks, tax jurisdictions, and report security.
3. Migrate opening balances and approved master data through controlled import batches.
4. Reconcile AP, AR, job cost, payroll, banking, fixed assets, tax, and WIP to signed control totals.
5. Execute parallel close and billing cycles.
6. Enable integrations incrementally with replay and rollback controls.
7. Activate AI in recommendation-only mode until accuracy and governance thresholds are met.
8. Complete UAT, security, performance, disaster recovery, and financial sign-off.
9. Lock the approved baseline and preserve deployment evidence.
