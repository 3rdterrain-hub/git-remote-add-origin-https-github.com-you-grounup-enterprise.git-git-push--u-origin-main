# GES Phase 10 - Enterprise Financial Management & Accounting

**Document family:** GrounUp Enterprise Specification (GES)  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Review-ready  
**Proposed lock tag:** `GES-P10-v1.0.0`

## Purpose
Phase 10 establishes the governed construction financial operating layer for GrounUp. It connects estimates, projects, contracts, workforce, procurement, equipment, billing, cash, and statutory records to an auditable financial ledger and a live Financial Digital Twin.

## Governance
Phase 10 may be locked only after Product, Finance, Project Controls, Payroll, Security, Architecture, Data Governance, and UAT approvals are recorded. Posted financial transactions are immutable and corrected only through governed reversal or adjustment.
