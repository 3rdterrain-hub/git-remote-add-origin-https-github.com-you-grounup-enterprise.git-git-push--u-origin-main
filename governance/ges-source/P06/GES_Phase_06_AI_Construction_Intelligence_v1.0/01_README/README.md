# GES Phase 06 - AI Construction Intelligence Platform

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Review-ready  
**Proposed lock tag:** GES-P06-v1.0.0

## Purpose
Phase 06 defines the governed AI brain of GrounUp: multi-agent orchestration, construction knowledge retrieval, multimodal document intelligence, human approval, explainability, security, evaluation, continuous improvement, white labeling, and marketplace extensibility.

## Package metrics
- 120 controlled requirements
- 20 specialist agents
- 20 AI engines
- 40 canonical entities
- 15 workflows
- 20 business rules
- 20 validation rules
- 24 API endpoints
- 12 UI surfaces
- 12 reports
- 120 mapped test cases

## Core design principle
AI may recommend, explain, compare, and prepare actions. Material decisions remain governed by policy, permissions, approvals, canonical APIs, and immutable audit history.
