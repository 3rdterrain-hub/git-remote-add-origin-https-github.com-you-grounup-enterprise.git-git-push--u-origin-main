# Phase 01 AI Governance

AI agents are governed identities. They must have explicit tenant, resource, action, tool, and data permissions.

Every material AI action records:
- requesting user or triggering workflow
- agent and model version
- tools invoked
- source evidence
- confidence and assumptions
- target object and proposed diff
- policy and authorization decision
- reviewer and approval outcome
- final writeback and audit correlation

AI may not bypass tenant isolation, explicit denial, segregation of duties, legal hold, locked baseline protection, or approval requirements.
