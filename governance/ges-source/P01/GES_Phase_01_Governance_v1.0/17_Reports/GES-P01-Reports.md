# Phase 01 Reports and Dashboards

- **RPT-P01-001 Governance Executive Summary** - Audience: Executive; Governance Admin. Tenant health, open risks, access exceptions, policy compliance, approval backlog, and emergency access
- **RPT-P01-002 Access Review Report** - Audience: Security Admin; Auditor. Users, roles, permissions, scopes, last activity, conflicts, and certification status
- **RPT-P01-003 Administrative Change Report** - Audience: Auditor. Configuration, role, policy, tenant, and administrative changes by period and actor
- **RPT-P01-004 Policy Compliance Report** - Audience: Compliance Manager. Active policies, acknowledgments, exceptions, overdue actions, and superseded versions
- **RPT-P01-005 Approval Performance** - Audience: Workflow Admin. Volume, cycle time, rejection, expiration, delegation, bottlenecks, and escalations
- **RPT-P01-006 Retention and Legal Hold** - Audience: Records Manager; Legal. Records due, disposed, suppressed, held, released, and exception reasons
- **RPT-P01-007 Emergency Access Review** - Audience: Security; Auditor. Requests, approvals, duration, actions, findings, and closure status
- **RPT-P01-008 AI Governance Activity** - Audience: AI Governance. Agent proposals, confidence, approvals, rejections, overrides, writebacks, and exceptions
