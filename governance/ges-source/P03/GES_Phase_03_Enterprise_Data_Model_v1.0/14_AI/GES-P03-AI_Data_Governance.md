# AI Data Governance

AI outputs are not authoritative business data until policy, authorization, version checks, and required approvals are satisfied. Every material AI-created or AI-modified value records the exact agent, model, prompt, tool calls, evidence, source versions, confidence, reviewer, decision, and committed writeback.

The canonical model separates:
- AI execution;
- AI recommendation;
- proposed AI writeback;
- approval decision;
- committed business record;
- feedback and outcome.
