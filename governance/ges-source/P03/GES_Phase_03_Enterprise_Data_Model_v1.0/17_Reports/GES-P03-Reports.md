# Phase 03 Reports

- **RPT-P03-001 Enterprise Data Model Inventory** - Domains, entities, fields, owners, classifications, and release versions
- **RPT-P03-002 Data Quality Scorecard** - Completeness, validity, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, and exception trends
- **RPT-P03-003 Master Data Duplicate Report** - Duplicate candidates, confidence, reasons, merge status, and unresolved risk
- **RPT-P03-004 Schema Change Impact** - Changed entities, fields, contracts, consumers, migrations, tests, and compatibility
- **RPT-P03-005 Import Reconciliation** - Source counts, accepted, rejected, merged, totals, lineage, and unresolved exceptions
- **RPT-P03-006 Lineage and Provenance Audit** - Origin, transformations, formulas, AI execution, evidence, and approving actors
- **RPT-P03-007 Retention and Legal Hold** - Retention schedules, eligible records, holds, archives, and disposition exceptions
- **RPT-P03-008 AI Writeback Activity** - Recommendations, approvals, rejections, commits, failures, evidence, and feedback
