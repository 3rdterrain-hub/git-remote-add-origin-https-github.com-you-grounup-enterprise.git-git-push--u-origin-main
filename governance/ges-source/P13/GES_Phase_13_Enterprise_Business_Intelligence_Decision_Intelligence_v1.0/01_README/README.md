# GES Phase 13 - Enterprise Business Intelligence, Executive Analytics & Decision Intelligence

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: Review-ready  
Proposed lock tag: GES-P13-v1.0.0

## Purpose
Phase 13 creates GrounUp's governed enterprise intelligence layer. It unifies estimating, projects, CRM, workforce, finance, procurement, fleet, safety, quality and future data domains into certified metrics, dashboards, forecasts, scenarios, AI-assisted insights and auditable decision records.

## Non-negotiable controls
1. Tenant isolation and role/attribute-based access.
2. Certified semantic metric definitions.
3. Source lineage and freshness visible at decision time.
4. AI recommendations include evidence and confidence.
5. Consequential AI recommendations require human approval.
6. Decision outcomes are recorded for continuous calibration.
