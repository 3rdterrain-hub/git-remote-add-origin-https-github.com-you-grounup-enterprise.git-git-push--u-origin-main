# Phase 18 Deployment Guide

1. Configure scheduling roles, project/portfolio profiles, calendars, codes and schedule quality rules.
2. Configure activity/WBS templates and baseline/update approval workflows.
3. Configure workforce, fleet, procurement, material and subcontractor resource interfaces.
4. Configure CPM calculation, critical/longest-path, float and constraint rules.
5. Configure progress-measurement methods and earned-value integration.
6. Configure look-ahead, Last Planner/PPC, pull planning and constraint workflows.
7. Configure resource loading, leveling, smoothing and portfolio-capacity rules.
8. Configure weather, scenario, schedule-risk, recovery and acceleration models.
9. Configure delay-event/fragnet/TIA evidence workflows without automating legal conclusions.
10. Import and reconcile representative Primavera/MS Project/CSV schedules.
11. Reconcile schedule/resource forecasts against Phase 07, 09, 10, 11, 12 and 13.
12. Validate AI schedule recommendations, evidence and human-review controls.
13. Complete Project Controls, Operations, Finance, Workforce, Fleet, Procurement, Architecture, Security, AI Governance and UAT approvals.
14. Release by controlled portfolio/project waves with rollback and baseline-protection checks.
