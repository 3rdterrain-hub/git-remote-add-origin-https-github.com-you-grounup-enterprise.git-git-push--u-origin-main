# Phase 19 Deployment Guide

1. Configure commercial roles, approval matrices, contract types, clause/obligation taxonomies and jurisdiction profiles.
2. Configure contract intake, versioning, amendment and clause review workflows.
3. Configure notice requirements, deadline calculation, delivery evidence and escalation.
4. Configure potential-change, pricing, T&M/force-account, authorization and change-order workflows.
5. Configure contract value, pending exposure, allowances, contingencies and forecasting.
6. Configure claims, chronology, delay/disruption, acceleration and differing-site-condition evidence workflows.
7. Configure payment applications, retainage, backcharges, escalation/price adjustments, insurance/bond and lien/waiver controls.
8. Integrate Phase 15 documents, Phase 18 schedules, finance/cost, procurement and subcontract commitments.
9. Validate segregation of duties and authority limits using representative contract/change scenarios.
10. Validate AI clause/notice/change/backup/risk assistance with evidence and human review.
11. Complete Commercial, Project Controls, Finance, Legal/Contracts, Operations, Architecture, Security, AI Governance, Data Governance and UAT approvals.
12. Release by controlled project waves with commercial reconciliation and rollback controls.
