## Non-negotiable controls
- Binding notices, waivers, settlements and executed commitments require authorized human action.
- Approved commercial versions are immutable and superseded through controlled revision.
- Notice/deadline rules retain their contract/jurisdiction basis and are configurable.
- Claims/delay modules preserve evidence and chronology without autonomously making legal conclusions.
- AI is advisory and evidence-grounded.
- Commercial values reconcile with cost/finance, schedule, procurement/subcontracts and document control.
