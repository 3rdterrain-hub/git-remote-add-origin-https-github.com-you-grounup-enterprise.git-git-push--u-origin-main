# Phase 14 Deployment Guide

1. Configure jurisdictions, companies, projects and HSEQ roles.
2. Load risk matrices, hazard/control libraries, training rules and inspection templates.
3. Configure stop-work, permit, competent-person and hold-point authorities.
4. Migrate open incidents, actions, permits, training and compliance obligations.
5. Validate mobile/offline workflows and evidence synchronization.
6. Validate notification/escalation rules.
7. Reconcile HSEQ analytics with Phase 13.
8. Run safety-critical negative testing and UAT.
9. Complete Security, HSEQ, Operations, Quality, Environmental and AI Governance approvals.
10. Release by controlled tenant/project waves with rollback plan.
