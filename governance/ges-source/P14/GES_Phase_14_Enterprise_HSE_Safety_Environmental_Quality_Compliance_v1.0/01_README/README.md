## Critical principles
- Safety-critical controls are human-authoritative.
- AI may assist with hazard recognition, classification, trends and recommendations but cannot override stop-work or regulated approval authority.
- Every critical decision preserves evidence, scope, responsible person and audit history.
- Field workflows are designed for mobile use and offline-capable implementation where configured.
