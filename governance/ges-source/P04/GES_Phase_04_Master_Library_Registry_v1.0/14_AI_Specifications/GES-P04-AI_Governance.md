# AI Specifications

The AI layer may retrieve only approved and accessible library versions. Every answer or automated action must preserve provenance, confidence, tenant scope and effective date. AI-generated proposals enter Draft status and cannot self-approve. Semantic duplicate detection may recommend merge, relationship or rejection, but a governed reviewer makes the final decision. White-label AI assistants inherit tenant terminology and visibility policies without exposing GrounUp internal branding where OEM mode is enabled.
